# Objective

Enhance the Election module by adding a secure video recording system during vote casting.

Whenever a voter casts a vote:

1. The user's webcam must automatically open.
2. Record a video of the voter during the voting session.
3. Limit the maximum upload size to **80 MB**.
4. Upload the recorded video directly to **Cloudinary** using the Cloudinary API.
5. Store only the Cloudinary URL and required metadata inside MongoDB.
6. Do NOT store videos in MongoDB.

The recording should become a permanent part of the voting process.

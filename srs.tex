\section{Problem Definition}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\subsection{Problem Statement}

The CSEDU Students' Club (CSEDUSC), formally constituted under the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Dhaka, currently lacks a centralised digital platform to manage its members, events, elections, finances, and committee structures. Key pain points include:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Manual, paper-based membership registration and tracking.
  \item No systematic mechanism to enforce eligibility rules for elections (e.g., year of study, prior EC experience).
  \item Absence of a transparent financial ledger accessible to moderators and the chief patron.
  \item Difficulty tracking executive committee attendance, meeting minutes, and quarterly expenditure reports.
  \item No structured process for certificate of membership requests.
\end{itemize}

Without a unified system, the club risks constitutional violations, reduced transparency, and administrative overhead -- ultimately hampering its mission to foster students' holistic growth through co-curricular and extra-curricular activities.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{tcolorbox}[significancestyle]
The absence of a centralised platform creates systemic inefficiencies. This SRS defines the requirements for a digital solution that enforces the CSEDUSC Constitution programmatically, ensuring transparency, accountability, and constitutional compliance across all club operations.
\end{tcolorbox}
\vspace{4pt}

\subsection{Goals and Non-Goals}

\textbf{\small\color{DeepNavy}GOALS}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Digitise membership registration and lifecycle management (including cancellation rules per Articles VI--VII).
  \item Automate eligibility checks for election candidacy per Article XIV and XV.
  \item Provide a transparent financial management module aligned with Article XII.
  \item Enable online executive meeting scheduling, attendance recording, and minute-keeping (Article XVI).
  \item Support an Annual General Meeting (AGM) workflow and election commission formation (Article XVIII).
  \item Provide certificate-of-membership request and issuance workflow (Article XIX).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\small\color{DeepNavy}NON-GOALS}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Integration with the university's academic result system.
  \item Full Learning Management System (LMS) or course delivery capabilities.
  \item Mobile-native (iOS/Android) application in v1.
  \item Automated financial transactions / online payment gateway in v1.
\end{itemize}

\section{Scope and Context}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\subsection{In Scope}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Member registration, profile management and membership-status tracking.
  \item EC formation, role assignment, impeachment, resignation and vacancy-filling workflows.
  \item Two-phase election module with eligibility validation.
  \item Event planning, volunteer management, and activity calendar aligned with Article XIII mandatory activities.
  \item Financial module: budget submission, fund tracking, quarterly expenditure reports and audit trail.
  \item Meeting scheduler, attendance recording.
  \item Constitution change proposal and approval workflow (Article XVII).
  \item AGM workflow and election commission formation.
  \item Certificate-of-membership request and issuance.
  \item Role-based access control (RBAC) for all user groups.
  \item Notification system (email) for meetings, elections, and notices.
  \item Online payment system for events and various payment.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Out of Scope}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Integration with university ERP or student information systems.
  \item Native mobile applications.
  \item Social-media auto-posting (only link sharing).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assumptions and Constraints}
\textbf{\small\color{Teal}ASSUMPTIONS}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item All members and executives have access to internet-enabled devices (desktop, laptop, or tablet) with modern web browsers.
  \item Users possess basic computer literacy and familiarity with web-based applications.
  \item The CSEDUSC Constitution remains stable during development; any amendments will be incorporated in subsequent releases.
  \item The university administration supports digital transformation of club operations and grants necessary permissions for system deployment.
  \item All financial records and member data will be maintained digitally with physical backups where legally required.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\small\color{Teal}TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textit{Platform Limitation:} The system must be delivered as a responsive web application accessible through standard web browsers; native mobile applications are excluded from the initial scope.
  \item \textit{Infrastructure:} University internet bandwidth may fluctuate (especially during peak hours); the application must remain functional with network low speed.
  \item \textit{Performance:} The system must handle at least 500 concurrent users during peak activity periods (e.g., election voting, event registration).
  \item \textit{Compatibility:} Must support desktop and tablet viewports; mobile optimization is secondary priority for v1.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\small\color{Teal}ORGANISATIONAL CONSTRAINTS}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item System must strictly enforce rules specified in the CSEDUSC Constitution.
  \item Chief Patron and Moderator must retain override/approval capabilities at all times.
  \item Membership cancellation notices require multi-party digital signatures.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\small\color{Teal}TIMELINE CONSTRAINTS}\vspace{4pt} 

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textit{Lab 2 (Current):} SRS v1, problem definition, user stories, wireframes, and initial data model must be completed and submitted for peer evaluation.
  
  \item \textit{Lab 3 (Peer Review):} SRS revision based on peer feedback must be completed before sprint development begins.
  
  \item \textit{Lab 4 (Sprint 1 -- MVP):} Working vertical slices delivering end-to-end functionality for at least three core modules (membership registration, basic event listing, and user authentication). SDD documentation and public repository link submission required. Target features:
  \begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Member registration and login system
    \item Role-based access control (RBAC) foundation
    \item Basic event creation and viewing
    \item Public portal homepage
  \end{itemize}
  
  \item \textit{Lab 5 (Sprint 2 -- Core Expansion):} Complete remaining core workflows with improved reliability and error handling. Automatic deployment pipeline operational. Target features:
  \begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Executive committee dashboard
    \item Event volunteer management
    \item Meeting scheduler and attendance tracking
    \item Financial module foundation (budget submission)
    \item Election management
  \end{itemize}
  
  \item \textit{Lab 6 (UI Review):} System must be deployed and accessible for peer usability/accessibility critique after two sprints of development.
  
  \item \textit{Lab 7 (Testing Baseline):} Unit, integration, and end-to-end test suite operational.
  
  \item \textit{Lab 8 (Peer QA):} Adversarial QA testing by peer teams; all critical bugs from Lab 6 UI review resolved.
  
  \item \textit{Lab 9 (Hardening):} Address critical and high-priority bugs from peer QA; finalize documentation, deployment runbook, and production readiness checklist.
  
  \item \textit{Lab 10 (Final Defense):} Fully functional system with live demo capability, comprehensive test evidence, final report, video demonstration, and complete GitHub repository with deployment instructions.
  
  \item \textit{External Constraint:} MVP (Labs 4--5) should align with CSEDUSC operations.  pilot deployment with 10--15 executive members during Lab 9 for real-world validation.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{\small\color{Teal}KEY CONSTRAINTS AND INTEGRITY RULES}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textit{Uniqueness:} Each Member has a unique \texttt{studentID}; each ECMember has a unique \texttt{post} per term (no duplicate posts).
  \item \textit{Referential Integrity:} All foreign keys (e.g., \texttt{voterID}, \texttt{candidateID}, \texttt{memberID}) must reference valid \texttt{Member} or \texttt{ECMember} records.
  \item \textit{Status Validation:} Member \texttt{status} transitions follow state machine: Active $\rightarrow$ \{Expired, Cancelled\}; no reverse transitions allowed.
  \item \textit{Election Phase Sequencing:} Phase 2 cannot start until Phase 1 results are published; votes are immutable after submission.
  \item \textit{Financial Audit Trail:} Every \texttt{Transaction} must have \texttt{category}, \texttt{amount > 0}, \texttt{timestamp}, and \texttt{createdBy} (Treasurer).
  \item \textit{Meeting Attendance:} Only current EC members can have attendance entries; absence count auto-increments for no-shows.
  \item \textit{Certificate Approval:} Certificates can only be issued by users with \texttt{role = Chief Patron}; one pending request per member.
  \item \textit{Term Boundaries:} EC terms are non-overlapping; archived EC records retain historical post assignments for eligibility checks.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{\small\color{Teal}DATA RETENTION POLICY}\vspace{4pt}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Active Records:} Member profiles, current EC data, ongoing elections, and pending transactions retained in primary database.
  \item \textbf{Historical Archives:} Past EC terms, completed elections, and closed financial years moved to archive tables after 1 year; retained for 7 years for audit purposes.
  \item \textbf{Deletion Policy:} Member data anonymized 3 years after graduation unless consent given for alumni engagement.
\end{itemize}
